[Index](../upstream-port-audit.md). During manual acceptance of the macOS
`3275293` package the user reported a vertical scroll bar flashing over the Add

Qt 6.12.0 `QAbstractScrollAreaPrivate::layoutChildren_helper/flashScrollBars` and
`QMacStyle::styleHint/pixelMetric/drawComplexControl` confirm that transient native
bars flash on opening and overlap the viewport. A local proxy style, owned by
the Add scroll area and applied only to its two bars, disables transient drawing
and overlap. It owns its separate base style, never the application style.
An adjacent local scroll-area adapter includes the gutter in the form's minimum
width when native/translated layout metrics change. This prevents a new narrow
horizontal scrollbar in Russian Cocoa. Both adapters are small presentation-only
classes in the existing form implementation; no operation logic is extracted or
reimplemented. Original source language, module boundaries and notices remain.
